QuickChat Mesh App
Zero-Network Offline Communication

QuickChat lets participating devices relay encrypted messages between nearby phones using Bluetooth & Multi-Hop mesh forwarding without cellular signal or Wi-Fi.

🏕️ How to use in No-Internet / Trekking Zones:
  1. Click Install App right now while you have internet.
  2. The app is 100% cached on your phone by the Service Worker.
  3. Go to any remote/disaster/no-network area and open the QuickChat icon.
  4. All nearby devices with QuickChat will automatically discover each other and exchange encrypted messages!
