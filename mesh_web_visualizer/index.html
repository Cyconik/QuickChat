Zero-Network Offline Communication

QuickChat turns every device into an Autonomous Mesh Relay Node. Even if the destination user is 5 km away, intermediate phones forward encrypted messages hop-by-hop automatically!

🌐 5 KM Multi-Hop Mesh Relay Architecture:
  1. Autonomous Hop Forwarding: Your phone acts as a relay medium. When Phone A sends to Phone D (5 km away), Phone B & Phone C pass the message forward instantly.
  2. Store-and-Forward (Mule Routing): If a person moves between two offline zones, their phone carries the encrypted message and delivers it upon arrival!
  3. Zero-Click Auto Discovery: Phones discover each other automatically in real-time without needing to press any scan button.
🔐 Cryptographic Security:
  • 🛡️ Ed25519 Elliptic Curve Keypairs for zero-trust cryptographic identity.
  • 🔒 AES-256-GCM End-to-End Encryption (Relay nodes cannot read message contents).
  • 📦 Loop-Free Routing with 8-hop TTL and deduplication cache.
