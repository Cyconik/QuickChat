QuickChat Mesh Groups
Zero-Internet Multi-Hop Bluetooth Broadcast

QuickChat connects phones into an automatic mesh chain: Phone A connects to B, B to C, C to D. Group messages propagate instantly to all members without any cellular tower, internet, or SIM card needed!

📡 How Offline Mesh Group Chat Works:
  1. Step 1: Open App: Native BLE radio discovers all nearby phones in range.
  2. Step 2: Join or Create Group: Enter #Nearby-Public-Mesh or create a custom group.
  3. Step 3: Infinite Multi-Hop Relay: Packets hop through intermediate phones seamlessly.
  4. Step 4: Chime & Alert: Every phone chimes 🔔 and updates the chat in real-time.
