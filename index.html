Zero-Internet A ➔ B ➔ C ➔ D Multi-Hop Forwarding

QuickChat forms an autonomous decentralized mesh chain: User A connects to B, B to C, C to D. Messages from A reach D seamlessly through intermediate phone relays!

📡 How Automatic Bluetooth Mesh Relaying Works:
  1. Step 1: Open App on Phones: QuickChat turns on Native BLE radio and discovers nearby phones automatically.
  2. Step 2: Type Message: Send direct message or chat in #Nearby-Public-Mesh.
  3. Step 3: Multi-Hop Relay (A ➔ B ➔ C ➔ D): Intermediate phones forward the Bluetooth radio packets to reachable nodes.
  4. Step 4: Instant Sound & Alert: Target phone chimes 🔔, vibrates, and confirms delivery receipt.
