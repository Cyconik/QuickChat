Step-by-Step Setup Guide

Follow these 4 simple steps to set up QuickChat and keep the background Bluetooth radio always active:

1
Step 1: Install APK & Close Recent Apps
Download and install QuickChat-Mesh-Offline.apk on your Android phone. Once installed, close all existing background apps and tabs from your phone's Recent Apps screen before opening QuickChat.
👉 App install karne ke baad turant open karne se pehle phone ke saare background apps/tabs close karein.
2
Step 2: Set Username & Display Name
Open QuickChat. Enter your chosen Display Name and Username handle (or tap 🎲 Shuffle to pick an instant 1-tap anonymous nickname), then tap Launch QuickChat Mesh 🚀.
👉 App open karein aur apna Name aur Username set karein. Koi phone number ya OTP ki zarurat nahi hai.
3
Step 3: Grant Bluetooth & Location Permissions
When prompted, grant Bluetooth / Nearby Devices and Location permissions so that Android allows the Native BLE Radio to continuously discover neighboring phones.
👉 Sabhi permissions (Bluetooth & Location) allow karein.
⚠️ Troubleshooting Note: If permission prompts do not appear on first launch, clear QuickChat from Recent Apps and reopen it, or go to Phone Settings ➔ Apps ➔ QuickChat ➔ Permissions and manually enable "Nearby Devices" and "Location".
4
Step 4: Lock App in Background (Most Important! 🔒)
To ensure you never miss any incoming group messages, keep QuickChat running in the background. If the app is swiped away or killed by Android, the Bluetooth radio stops listening.
👉 App ko background me ON rakhein taaki continuous group messages receive hote rahein!
🔒 How to Lock App: Open your phone's Recent Apps screen, long-press or tap the lock icon / 3 dots on the QuickChat card, and select Lock App (🔒). Also set Battery Usage to "Unrestricted / Don't Optimize" so Android doesn't kill the mesh radio in sleep mode.

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Does QuickChat require any internet, Wi-Fi or SIM card?
No! QuickChat is 100% offline and communicates strictly through direct Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) radio packets between phones.
❓ What is the communication range?
Direct BLE range is typically 30 to 50 meters per phone. With Multi-Hop Relaying (A ➔ B ➔ C ➔ D), the range expands continuously across entire kilometers as more people run the app.
❓ Why do I need to lock the app in background?
Android's battery manager aggressively terminates background Bluetooth radios when apps are closed. Locking QuickChat in your Recent Apps screen keeps the radio listening continuously for incoming group messages.
