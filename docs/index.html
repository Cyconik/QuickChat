QuickChat Mesh App
Zero-Network Offline Communication

QuickChat lets participating devices relay encrypted messages between nearby phones using Bluetooth & Multi-Hop mesh forwarding without cell towers or Wi-Fi.

🏕️ How to use in No-Internet / Trekking Zones:
  1. Click Install App (or Add to Home Screen) right now while connected.
  2. Once installed, the PWA is 100% cached on your device by our Service Worker.
  3. Go to any remote/disaster/no-network area, open the QuickChat icon from your home screen.
  4. All nearby users with QuickChat installed will automatically discover each other and exchange encrypted messages!
🔒 Security & Multi-Hop Architecture

• Zero-Knowledge Relay: Intermediate nodes (A → B → C) forward encrypted packets without ability to read content.
• End-to-End Encryption: Secured with AES-256-GCM and Ed25519 digital signatures.
• Store & Forward: Messages buffer locally when a receiver is out of range and auto-deliver when in proximity.
