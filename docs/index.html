App Installation & Setup Guide (Crucial)

Follow these steps carefully to ensure your phone continuously listens to the mesh and never drops incoming group chats:

1
Install APK & Close Recent Apps First
Download and install QuickChat-Mesh-Offline.apk. Once installed, close all existing background tabs and recent apps before opening the app for the first time.
2
Set Username & Display Name
Open QuickChat. Pick your preferred Display Name and Username handle (or tap 🎲 Shuffle to pick a quick 1-tap anonymous nickname), then tap Launch QuickChat Mesh 🚀.
3
Grant All Required Permissions (Bluetooth & Location)
Allow Bluetooth / Nearby Devices and Location permissions so the Native BLE Mesh radio can discover neighboring phones.
⚠️ Permission Troubleshooting: If the permission prompt doesn't show up, clear the app from Recent Apps and reopen it, or go to Phone Settings ➔ Apps ➔ QuickChat ➔ Permissions and enable "Nearby Devices" and "Location".
4
Keep App in Background & Lock in Recent Apps 🔒
To ensure you never miss any incoming group messages, do NOT swipe away the app from your Recent Apps list. Keep QuickChat running in the background.
🔒 Pro Tip (Lock App): Open your phone's Recent Apps screen, long-press or tap the 3 dots on the QuickChat card, and select Lock App (🔒). Also set Battery Usage to "Unrestricted / Don't Optimize" so Android doesn't kill the Bluetooth mesh radio.
